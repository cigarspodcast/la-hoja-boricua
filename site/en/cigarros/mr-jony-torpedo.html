Mr. Jony Torpedo

By Mr. Jony Cigars · Caguas, Puerto Rico

Wrappers: Habano, Broadleaf Maduro, Connecticut and Habano Ecuador; also Barber Pole (Connecticut + Maduro). Nicaraguan filler and an Ecuadorian binder. It started with full-strength blends and later added milder ones (Humo Latino).
