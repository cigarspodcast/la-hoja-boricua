LHB-39: Mi Hoja stats and a searchable diary, in the #/mihoja/diario view of site/hoja.js. Pinned decisions (binding): stats are total catas, passport stamps by kind (artesanos, cigarros, lugares), top-rated cigars, and the most-logged capa and fortaleza; the diary is searchable by name, marca, artesano and tag, filterable by stars and by public/private, and sortable by date or stars. No backend.
1. diaryStats(s,d): catas counts diary entries that are objects (the stored diary can hold null or strings, which normalize() keeps); sellos counts pasaporte ids per kind that are still in the catalog (d.artesanos, d.cigarros, d.places), the same rule as passCounts() in the Pasaporte view; top is the three best cigars by average stars over rated catas (1 to 5), a catalog cigar keyed by cigarId and an "otro" cigar by its folded name (dfold: accents, case and spaces), ties by number of catas then name; capa and fortaleza are the most frequent non-empty values, ties alphabetical.
2. diaryFilter(list,q): q.q is split into words and every word must appear (after dfold) in the name, marca, artesano or a tag (its stored key or its shown name WN(t)); q.st keeps exactly that many stars; q.vis 'pub' or 'priv'; q.ord 'fecha' sorts by the day smoked (consumido, else the date of fecha) newest first, ties by fecha; 'estrellas' by stars, ties by that date order. It returns a new array and does not reorder the stored diary.
3. vDiario renders the stats panel, then (when the diary has entries) the filter form, a count line and #dList. A filter change repaints only #dList and the count (so typing keeps focus); a full repaint (delete, sync arrival) keeps the query (module DQ, keyed by the account's sub, so another account starts clean) and returns focus and the caret to the search box if it had focus. The entry card markup moved unchanged into dCard; the delete and public handlers moved into bindDiario and now skip non-object entries.
4. site/i18n.js: new es/en keys (h.st*, h.dq*); site/styles.css: rules for .dstats, .dst-*, .dfilt, .dq-row, #dqN. site/sw.js is not bumped yet (that happens at merge, one above main). test/site.test.js: HOJA38's harness takes opts.D (catalog) and opts.api (extra exports); three LHB-39 tests run the real code on a fixture document.
