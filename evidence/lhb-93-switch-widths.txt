Method: getBoundingClientRect().width of #langSwitch after setting its innerHTML to each label wire() writes.
Counting basis: CSS pixels of the button box, border and padding included (styles.css *{box-sizing:border-box}).

400 px and wider (full name, padding 0 12px):   "English" 69.9   "Espanol" (Español) 73.7
under 400 px (code, padding 0 10px):            "EN" 39.9        "ES" 37.7
empty (the button as main ships it, before wire() fills it): 25.6

Before this change the button shipped hidden and empty; wire() (DOMContentLoaded, after the deferred scripts) un-hid it
and filled it, so the 70 to 74 px button appeared after first paint and moved the controls beside it (Lighthouse: a 0.007
shift of div.top-actions in 4 of 5 runs, evidence/lhb-90-perf.txt).
After: index.html ships it visible with the Spanish-default content ("English" / "EN", lang en, aria-label English),
which is exactly what wire() writes for a Spanish reader, so nothing changes for them; for an English reader wire()
writes "Español" / "ES". min-width 74px (and 40px under 400 px) holds the wider of the two, so the swap does not move
the neighbours. Visible change: a Spanish reader's button grows from 69.9 to 74 px at 400 px and wider; under 400 px it shows EN at
39.9 px, which min-width 40 leaves as is. An English reader's button is unchanged at 400 px and wider (73.7 to 74).
Under 400 px an English reader's ES grows from 37.7 to 40 px.
