LHB-39: Mi Hoja stats and a searchable diary, in the #/mihoja/diario view of site/hoja.js. Pinned decisions (binding): stats are total catas, passport stamps by kind (artesanos, cigarros, lugares), top-rated cigars, and the most-logged capa and fortaleza; the diary is searchable by name, marca, artesano and tag, filterable by stars and by public/private, and sortable by date or stars.
