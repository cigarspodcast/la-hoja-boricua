## Findings (each becomes its own Backlog ticket)

1. target-size, 23 elements on /#/lugares: the map markers (div.leaflet-marker-icon) overlap, so the free target area of many is below 24 by 24 px (as small as 1 by 1 px). Basis: a11y-app-lugares run.
2. label-content-name-mismatch, 1 element on each of / and /#/lugares: the brand link a.brand in header.topbar has visible text that is not part of its accessible name. Lighthouse scored both pages anyway (100 and 96); the rule is in the run but not weighted. Basis: a11y-home and a11y-app-lugares runs.
3. aria-dialog-name, 1 element on /#/mihoja: the Clerk sign-in modal (div.cl-modalContent, role dialog) has no accessible name. The modal is Clerk's component, so the fix may be a Clerk option, or a label added after it mounts. Basis: a11y-app-mihoja run.
No contrast failures were found on the five pages in the dark theme (color-contrast passed on each).
Not covered: the signed-in Mi Hoja screens (no session in the audit), and a standalone axe-core run.
