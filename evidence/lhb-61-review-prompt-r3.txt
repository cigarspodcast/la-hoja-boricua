Round 2 found two blockers and one evidence defect. What changed:
1. P1, a NUL in a key. functions/_lib/feed.js no longer reads any member by a '$.key' path.
   - member(obj, key, pick) is a subquery over json_each(obj) with x.key = 'key' ORDER BY x.id DESC LIMIT 1, so the key must match whole and, as JSON.parse, the last of a repeated key wins. Measured with node:sqlite 3.53.4: for {"publica\u0000x":true,"publica":false}, json_type(v,'$.publica') is 'true', while json_each's key = 'publica' matches only the real key (type false).
   - DIARIO: the last top-level "diario" member when it is an array, of a document that is valid JSON, else '[]'.
   - PUBLICA: the last "publica" member is of type true.
   - DAY: the last "consumido" member when it is text of exactly 10 bytes (length(CAST(x.value AS BLOB)) = 10) that GLOBs as YYYY-MM-DD, else ''.
   - Second check: the parsed entry is skipped unless e.publica === true (publicDoc's rule).
   - Mi Hoja stores JSON.stringify(b.doc) (functions/_lib/mihoja.js), so a repeated key cannot be stored in production; a NUL inside a key can.
2. P2, the pinned 20 per page. A group bigger than PAGE is now split 20 at a time (groupPage).
   - The cursor inside such a group is "day~handle~n", with n the place of the last entry among the group's public entries counted from the oldest (1 is the oldest). Counting from the oldest keeps a new entry (unshifted to the front of the diary) from moving the others.
   - The next page of the group is its entries with a smaller n; after the last part, next is "day~handle" when anything follows, else null.
   - A three-part cursor whose group shrank returns what is left (possibly nothing) and goes on after the group.
   - Accepted limit, escalated to the owner on the ticket: inside a group bigger than a page, an older entry that turns public between two loads can skip another. Fixing it needs either a cursor that reveals the count of private entries, an entry id in the cursor, or a signed cursor (a new secret); the pinned page size and the privacy of the cursor rank above it. Outside such groups, the whole-group paging of round 1 still applies.
