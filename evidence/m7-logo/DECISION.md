## Why Parada

All three candidates are on the contact sheet, `evidence/m7-logo/contact-sheet.png`:

- **Parada** keeps a solid, readable shape at 16 and 32 px. Its road and its stop dot stay separate at every size (the LHB-66 cluster count, `evidence/m7-logo/clusters.txt`). In the header it reads as a leaf at a glance.
- **Ruta** is drawn as a line. At 16 px its outline and veins merge into a smudge.
- **Camino** puts the stop dot below and outside the leaf. Small, the dot reads as a stray mark rather than the end of the road.
